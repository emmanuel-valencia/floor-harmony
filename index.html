Frequently Asked Questions

Do you move furniture? +
Yes, we can move basic furniture as an added service for a simple fee. We require homeowners to remove all small breakables, electronics, and clear the tops of surfaces before our crew arrives.
Do you haul away the old carpet? +
Absolutely. Our tear-out service includes ripping up your old flooring, rolling it up, and hauling it away for proper disposal so you don't have to worry about a thing.
How long does installation usually take? +
Most standard residential installations (3-4 bedrooms and a hallway) are completed in a single day by our experienced in-house crew. Custom stairs or massive whole-home projects may require two days.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes. Floor Harmony LLC is fully licensed and insured to operate within the state of Colorado, protecting both your property and our family crew during every project.
