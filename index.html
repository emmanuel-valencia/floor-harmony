Flooring 101

Know Your Floors

Replacing your floors is a major investment. Most showrooms use confusing terminology to upsell you. At Floor Harmony, we believe an educated homeowner is our best client. Here is exactly what matters when choosing your new floors.

Talk to an Expert

1. Carpet Face Weight

This is the amount of yarn used in one square yard of carpet. Higher face weight generally means a denser, softer, and more durable carpet. We recommend heavy nylon for high-traffic living rooms.

2. The Importance of Padding

A great carpet feels terrible if installed over cheap padding. We strictly use high-density, spill-resistant padding to extend the life of your carpet and provide that "walking on clouds" feel.

3. LVP Waterproofing

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) is perfect for kitchens and basements because of its rigid core. It mimics the texture of authentic hardwood but is 100% waterproof and scratch-resistant against pets.
